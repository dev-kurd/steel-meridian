About

Steel Meridian is an original real-time strategy game. The two armies, Azure and Crimson, are fictional. The theatre map is a simplified sketch used to pick a mission, not an accurate map.

Left click / drag — select units or a structure
Right click — move, attack, or set a rally point · Ctrl+right click attack-move
Double click — select all of that unit type on screen
Ctrl+A — select the whole army · . or Tab next idle unit
X stop · Z hold position · V patrol · C guard
With a structure selected: 1…9 press its buttons · U upgrade
Ctrl+S save · the game also autosaves; use Continue on the main menu
Ctrl+1…9 / 1…9 — set and recall groups
Arrows / WASD / edges / middle drag — pan · wheel zoom
Q air strike · E repair · R fighters · T laser · B build turret pad
H home · Space last alert · P pause · F speed · M sound
Factories build units for free — pick what they make. Spend credits on upgrades, defences and structures.
Park ground units beside a neutral or knocked-out structure to capture it.
Each mission shows its objective at the top of the screen. Losing your Command Core always loses the mission.
Battle tanks and artillery cannot hit aircraft; Skyguards and most turrets can.
The Vanguard is your command craft, docked on the Command Core (G selects it). Sit it on a neutral or knocked-out structure to capture it, on one of your own to repair it, or send it to wreck enemy structures. If it is lost, a new one appears at the Core. Some missions have no Vanguard.
