B. Damaged look: current against the tank style

The new look uses the tank damage shader on the walker models: large black soot with a smoky tint around it, cracked plates, and only a few embers. "New, smokier" goes further: the smoky tint is stronger and reaches wider, the cracks are weaker and there are fewer embers. Flames and smoke are not in these pictures; the game adds them from the effects sheet.
